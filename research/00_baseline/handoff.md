## Key Findings

- AE-5 driving power is not considered the primary limitation of the current system.
- H7 TE passive analog behavior is the relevant comparison basis, not its internal USB DSP mode.
- The main desired improvements are resolution, natural mids/vocals, bass quality, soundstage, treble refinement and overall versatility.
- H7 TE comfort is a strength and should not be sacrificed excessively.
- Competitive FPS, wireless features and microphone quality are low-priority factors.
