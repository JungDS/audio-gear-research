## Four core finalist roles

### C001 — Sennheiser HD 560S: minimum-risk entry for open-back vocals and music

- **Price seen:** 189,000 KRW Schezade genuine/importer-identified October 7–31 event; 216,000 KRW older Danawa genuine category fallback. June/July 189,000 KRW promotions were previously recorded.
- **Working purchase threshold:** about **200,000 KRW during event**, re-evaluate if not available.
- **Why here:** natural, more consistent midrange than H7 passive's recessed presence; opening stage and generally balanced sound without paying for low-value FPS/mic/wireless features. Good independently measured evidence.
- **Why not:** open leakage, less isolated cinematic bass than H7, possible bright upper treble/clamp, and **seller sale says no non-defect change-of-mind exchange or refund**.
- **Step11 challenge:** compare H7 matched volume and HD550 on vocals and real game/film tracks, physically try first. Is the sound change beneficial enough to surrender closed-back isolation?

### C015 — FiiO FT1: closed-back movie and private listening

- **Price seen:** genuine-category indexed 215,670–230,000 KRW; 207,000 KRW is card-conditional and must not be used as ordinary price.
- **Working purchase threshold:** **230,000 KRW** for a new Korean-supported unit and known post-fix batch.
- **Why here:** retain H7's closed isolation and bass energy while plausibly improving bass extension and some tonal clarity; good counterpart rather than direct competitor to HD560S.
- **Why not:** still warm/bassy, so vocal improvements might not be dramatic. Manufacturer FiiO confirmed early-pivot screw/adhesive QC problem, says revised from manufacture on 2024-11-20; later isolated owner problems cannot establish new batch defect rate.
- **Step11 challenge:** Korean warranty and repair terms in writing, exact production lot/new hardware, earpad eyeglasses seal and two-hour wear; distinguish sound improvement from bass increase.

### C016 — FiiO FT1 PRO: open planar for a different tonal/technical presentation

- **Price seen:** Korean genuine-category indexed 312,930–313,790 KRW; sellers around 317,000 KRW. Card-only 285,300 KRW excluded from normal reference and cheaper import variant has different warranty.
- **Working purchase threshold:** around **320,000 KRW** genuine, seller-authorized and post-fix stock.
- **Why here:** relatively measured even midrange, planar driver with good low-distortion findings, credible difference from H7 bass-forward passive response. Different from warmer FT1 rather than unambiguously its 'upgraded version'.
- **Why not:** open leakage and reduced H7 movie impact; ~374g; same FiiO pivot-issue early batches and manufacturer-described rare planar low-bass rattle.
- **Step11 challenge:** QC serial/date, safe-volume worn low-frequency passage noise, direct HD560S audition with volume matched, ear/head comfort and parts service. If no meaningful sound gain over 560S, premium not warranted.

### C003 — Sennheiser HD 600: natural vocal / acoustic reference (conditional availability)

- **Price seen:** October 315,000 KRW Schezade event **marked SOLD OUT** in event overview even though individual page still displays buy controls. Danawa ~332,800 KRW previous indexed listing, Enuri 359,000 KRW September refresh, department-store normal offer over 400k.
- **Working purchase threshold:** around **360,000 KRW** for actually available new, Korean genuine stock; not a promise 315k can be purchased.
- **Why here:** unusually well documented natural midrange and voice/instrument timbre; long-running maintainable reference distinct from cinematic/planar choices.
- **Why not:** subbass and stage limits for movies; pad freshness and 300Ω AE-5 drive volume margin need physical check; higher price does not prove greater resolution over 560S.
- **Step11 challenge:** confirm actual seller stock and warranty, test AE-5 output headroom with representative peaks, compare vocals and bass to 560S and future 329k HD650 alternative if restocked.

## Why these models, why not others

- Core roles are nonduplicative: affordable open vocal/midrange, cinematic/private closed, technically different open planar, established vocal-acoustic reference.
- "All-around best" is **not declared**. Some people prefer FT1's isolation and bass to more expensive open headphones; others prefer natural HD600 vocal tone.
- HD550 and K371 are alternatives conditional on tonal preference, not evidence of massive incremental performance superiority.
- Premium models stay eligible without pretending rising prices equal rising measured resolution.
- A *hard REJECT* requires both comparative price and independently supported inferior user-relevant sound/comfort with no compensating advantage. This stage does not meet that burden for any new candidates; 62 IDs retained.
- H7 head-to-head resolution, dynamics, imaging remain UNKNOWN in project evaluations. Step10 must not fake quantification.
