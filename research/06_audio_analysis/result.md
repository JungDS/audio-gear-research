## Priority-model findings

- **HD 560S**: generally balanced open-back, lean deep bass, suitable as reference; treble impressions/revision vary.
- **HD 550**: somewhat fuller and mildly bright compared with 560S; DIY-Audio-Heaven and SoundGuys broadly positive, improvements may be modest.
- **HD 600**: neutral/natural midrange, good stereo matching and relatively low distortion, with weaker bottom-octave bass.
- **ATH-R50x**: excellent measured channel matching and mids; significant measured 6–10kHz treble rise makes perceived 'detail' and fatigue especially important.
- **ATH-R70xa**: balanced, natural voiced open-back; independent reviewers differ about high-treble level. Check 470Ω AE-5 headroom.
- **MDR-M1**: deep, full sealed bass but presence/recessed upper mids can make voices laid back (supported by two independent measurement/review sources).
- **MDR-MV1**: wide-open presentation, bass surprisingly full for open headphones, but independent sources warn about 5–9kHz sibilance.
- **FiiO FT1**: warm closed-back, strong bass, seal/glasses-dependent variation; distinguish bass level from bass quality.
- **FiiO FT1 PRO**: more neutral, open planar, low reported distortion and good midrange; no direct superiority inference over closed FT1.
- **aune AR5000 MK2**: a first independent measurement finds warm midbass, broad stage and some relative midrange tradeoff; lack of second rig makes confidence LOW. Published impedances disagree (28Ω vs 64Ω), leave unresolved.
- **HD 505 Copper**: promising balanced open-back with good vocal clarity; independent reviewers differ about degree of improvement over HD560S.
- **Sony INZONE H6 Air**: passive wired open gaming model reviewed independently in 2026, strong general gaming/dialogue case; shared MDR-MV1 driver family is not proof of identical tuning.

## Evidence disagreements and limits

- Headphones.com finds ATH-R70xa treble tastefully lively while MusicRadar describes it as relatively restrained.
- RTINGS rates Audeze MM-100 response/matching positively while SoundGuys is more critical of its mid-focused musical balance.
- FiiO FT3 32Ω and 350Ω versions have distinct driver construction and tuning; no single acoustic judgment covers both.
- HD490 PRO pad options materially alter response. Shure 'A' models must not inherit older SRH440/SRH840 measurements.
- Corsair VIRTUOSO PRO is genuinely passive-wired open-back, not automatically excluded as DSP-only gaming equipment.
- Different measurement targets, couplers, ear geometry, seal and listening levels prevent blind cross-lab numeric averaging.
