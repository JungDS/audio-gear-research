## Source caveats

- **Sennheiser HD 490 PRO (C004):** Producing vs Mixing pads give materially different tuning. The RTINGS and DIY-Audio-Heaven data are not a single universal sound profile; choose pad before comparison.
- **beyerdynamic DT 900 PRO X (C006):** full lower mids and 4kHz notch with uneven high treble; the narrower notches depend on ear transfer and measurement rig. Not automatically inferior to cheaper bright references.
- **ATH-R30x (C012):** independent review notes difference between measured apparent bass and personally perceived bass. More limited imaging/precision than other R-series does not make it worthless at lower cost.
- **FiiO FT3 (C017):** 32Ω and 350Ω are **different driver materials and tuning**, explicitly confirmed by FiiO. An unspecified FT3 product row cannot receive a definitive stage score. Exact model/impedance/earpads must be pinned in Step 7 or before Step 8.
- **FiiO FT5 (C018):** rich warmer/darker planar style; whether this is comfortable or lacking treble information is listening preference rather than a universal measurement verdict.
- **AKG K371 (C024):** very extended subbass if sealed; bass can vary on glasses. Not enough to describe the entire harmonic/treble texture simply as neutral.
- **Shure SRH440A and SRH840A (C027/C028):** only first-hand pairwise magazine review and official support found in this pass; do not substitute older **SRH440 / SRH840** measurements without the A suffix. Independent calibrated lab comparison remains missing.
- **Meze 105 AER (C030):** Headfonics describes controlled smooth treble; Pragmatic Audio notes somewhat more sharp/airy treble with different rig. Different emphasis and fit are relevant.
- **aune SR7000 (C033):** independent reviewers agree on unusual midrange clarity for a closed-back but differ on whether forward upper-mid rise could be harsh.
- **Audeze MM-100 (C037):** RTINGS broadly positive on response, channel matching and distortion; SoundGuys criticizes mid-focused, less satisfying overall listening. Specific score transfer to user's system is inappropriate.
- **Focal Azurys/Hadenys (C039/C040):** both offer premium passive headphone sound profiles with relaxed/rounded notes. Their higher prices do not prove higher resolution. Hadenys has strong musicality/comfort reports but detail can be judged merely good, not extraordinary.
- **Austrian Audio Hi-X65 (C041):** high clarity but a risk of perceived sibilance and some conflicting impedance/weight specifications across review sources; verify before AE-5 electrical assumption.
- **Corsair VIRTUOSO PRO (C044):** actual 3.5mm passive open headphone with credible measured midrange and stage; second link in matrix is *the same RTINGS provider* and cannot be counted as independent corroboration.
- **ASUS ROG Kithara (C052):** independent RTINGS and SoundGuys show a real studio-oriented open planar profile; expensive but not simply a gaming headset DSP purchase. Bass and large headset weight are tradeoffs, not sound ranking.
- **AKG K361 (C060):** RTINGS review plus RTINGS comparison is *one independent lab*, not two. Further non-RTINGS measurements required for high-confidence verdict.

## User-use implications

For music and voice-heavy anime, midrange and upper treble naturalness need more weight than a single resolution adjective. General gaming calls for stable positioning and usable bass, not just FPS-oriented features. Film requires separating cinematic subbass weight from bass quality and vocal intelligibility. Stage impression is partly subjective: open-back form is not itself proof of precise imaging.
