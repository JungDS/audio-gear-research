## Important findings

1. The initial 15-brand research map was a starting point, not an exhaustive market boundary.
2. Excluding all gaming brands would hide products such as Corsair VIRTUOSO PRO, which support direct analog use without relying on gaming DSP.
3. Consumer ANC headphones have distinct value propositions and power/analog differences; they must not be assumed superior or inferior to wired hi-fi products before comparison.
4. Recent versions matter: Bose QuietComfort 2nd Gen (2026), newer JBL LIVE 780NC succeeding 770NC, and Sennheiser HD 505 (2025) deserve explicit lifecycle review.
5. Some new candidates are expensive upper references and might never be financially sensible; this is for Step 4–9 to establish, not Step 2.
