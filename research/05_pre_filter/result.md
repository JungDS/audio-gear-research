## Objectives and what changed

The user considers a high-priced but clearly better-sounding headset/headphone worth keeping as a future purchase option. However, products which deliver demonstrably inferior audio/relevant usability at the same or higher genuine Korean cost should not consume full analysis budgets.

Screening rule:
- Do not use sticker price, MSRP, brand name or driver size as exclusion.
- Compare equal use cases and roughly equivalent **new/genuine vs import/refurb/warranty** terms.
- REJECT for poor performance-for-price only if **at least two independent apt sources** or comparably strong measurement + reliable first-hand comparison establish clear weakness in relevant listening dimensions against named cheaper alternative, **without a compensating meaningful strength**.
- If a source uses wireless DSP while user's reference is AE-5 3.5mm analog, do not treat the results as one-to-one.
- If low prices are indexed/nonlive or normal price UNKNOWN, do not claim exact dominance.
- Defer inconclusive cases and preserve reentry trigger.

## AE-5 compatibility and technical limitations

- Standard passive **3.5mm** wired headphones generally fit the AE-5 listening architecture, often with an adapter for 6.3mm or a detachable cable.
- **Maxwell 2**: manufacturer confirms 3.5mm AUX and USB, so do not falsely reject it as purely wireless-only (E0221).
- **Arctis Nova 7 Wireless**: manufacturer confirms battery-free analog 3.5mm listening, but older Gen1 inventory and domestic price are uncertain (E0222).
- **Bose QC 2nd Gen**: requires power even via cable; mixing its internal electronics with passive AE-5 amplifier output changes the comparison (E0216).
- **High-impedance / low-sensitivity candidates** (ATH-R70xa 470Ω; HD 6XX 300Ω; some planars): AE-5 supports a broad impedance range, but max level/headroom cannot be assumed from impedance alone. Verify source sensitivity and desired SPL before final compatibility verdict.
- Open-back leakage/isolation tradeoffs are not automatic disqualifiers for indoor listening.
- No candidate was technically REJECTED solely by a generic driver/sensitivity/frequency specification.
