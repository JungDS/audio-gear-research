## Executive findings

**Most cost-efficient first upgrade investigation:** **Sennheiser HD 560S C001, ₩189,000 during October 7–31 2026 promotion**, with matching historical June/July ₩189,000 retailer sales. This repeatedly observed sale price materially reduces the cost of testing a predictable vocal-balance upgrade over Creative H7 TE 3.5mm analog + AE-5. The official importer is identified by the Korean retailer page and purchase controls are displayed, but **cart completion and real-time allocation are NOT verified**. H7 relative change is synthesized, not controlled A/B.

**Lowest nominal sticker price is not the lowest reliable upgrade cost.** Shure SRH440A C027 ~₩157,440 and ATH-R30x C012 ~₩173,880 look cheaper from older indexed shopping prices, but the H7 replacement sonic benefit is weakly evidenced; R30x official manufacturer page presently shows SOLD OUT. A lower checkout price does not automatically mean a worthwhile H7 upgrade.

**Conditional value band:** approximately **₩189,000–₩320,000**, within which compelling but different use-case frontiers appear:
- **C001 HD 560S ~189k:** strong evidence for midrange/open presentation per cost. Low-bass and isolation penalty relative to H7.
- **C015 FiiO FT1 ~216–230k:** preservation of closed-back privacy and cinematic weight, different tonal target, but manufacturer-confirmed early batch pivot fastener QC risk; verify post-fix build and Korean aftersales.
- **C002 HD 550 ~254k:** newer/full-bodied and differently voiced open option; +65k over HD560S special can be justified by subjective preference, not proven technical superiority.
- **C010 ATH-R50x ~249–269k:** lightweight reference open headphone; potential treble sharpness and 55mm inner cup fit risk mean it is not automatically better than 560S.
- **C024 AKG K371 ~235k:** closed neutral reference alternative to warmer FT1; seal and serviceability matter.
- **C016 FiiO FT1 PRO ~313–317k:** open planar with verified measurement/tonal strengths, possible qualitative step in spatial/vocal experience, with factory-recognized early QC caveats.
- **C003 HD600:** a valuable vocal/timbre reference; **Oct ₩315k shown SOLD OUT**, so use ~₩332,800 old genuine-category comparison or ₩449,000 retailer catalog as **non-live context**, not pretend an available 315k buy.

**No evidence-backed hard monetary ceiling.** Above approximately **₩350k–₩500k**, additional cost has *less convincingly demonstrated* proportional H7 improvement in available independent material. Above ~₩500k specialty music/build/comfort preferences could still justify spending, but a measurable incremental upgrade of the same scale is not established. This is a **research-effort escalation threshold, not a user budget ceiling or a performance plateau claim**. No luxury product was hard-rejected merely for being costly.

## Price confidence and time-window design

- `Step4_Normal_KRW` comes from earlier Stage4 cross-portal typical advertised-price estimates, **only seven total models**. UNKNOWN elsewhere.
- `Step4_Listed_Low_KRW` is the older indexed/aggregated asking price and can be cached for months. It is neither a real-time checkout nor a guaranteed genuine authorized dealer.
- `Current_Effective_Reference_KRW` is a **scenario reference** for broad price comparisons: a dated credible promotion or pre-existing indexed new-product offer. **Not a universal live quote**. Individual recency and status are in price_refresh.csv.
- `Price_Confidence` is the reliability of source/format, **not a stock guarantee**.
- Conditional credit-card or membership rates remain separate from baseline. Imported, refurb, open-box and sold-out listings must never masquerade as genuine sealed-new offers.
- HD560S promo has recurred in June, July and October 2026, so it is not merely a one-off sighting, but recurring offer ≠ continuous 189k market norm.
- HD550 254k event and HD600 315k sold-out event require expiration/stock checks (Oct31). Event price must not be extrapolated into November.
- Focal Azurys ~664k and Hadenys ~766.5k are **open public long-running catalog discounts**. Their crossed-out MSRP/list figures are not real purchase-price normals.

## Value tiers and user-relevant tradeoffs

### A. Minimum credible improved H7 sound: approx ₩189k
**C001 HD 560S** is the first most defensible model based on *available comparable evidence*, rather than one of the lower-priced but less validated Shure/ATH models. Its more even voice presentation addresses the H7 passive mids, but open leakage and cinema impact differ. If the sale ends, re-evaluate against normal retail (~216–229k historical observation).

**Not yet a proven A/B upgrade:** the H7 + AE-5 exact audio improvement was not tested in a matched listening experiment.

### B. High-value multidirectional choice: approx ₩215k–₩320k
- Closed-back privacy + movies: FT1, K371.
- Vocal music/general use + open stage: HD 560S, HD 550, ATH-R50x.
- Different open-planar refinement/pad/QC tradeoff: FT1 PRO.
- Very light story gaming + voices: Sony INZONE H6 Air ~246k indexed.
- HD505 ~236k falls in between current 560S sale and 550 sale with no strongly demonstrated unique sonic advantage; a **conditional lower research priority**, not rejected.
- C012 ATH-R30x ~174k indexed appears only ~15k cheaper than 560S sale but is a weaker evidenced upgrade; further costly work is less valuable.

### C. Premium zone: >₩320k, particularly ₩420k–₩500k
- **HD 600** only after *actual new stock price* verified; distinguished voice/timbre benchmark.
- **ATH-R70xa ~429k:** 470Ω and cup swivel, explicit fit + amp gate.
- **beyerdynamic DT900 PRO X ~425k:** robust serviceable headband and pads may matter, but extra cost vs 560S not currently matched by demonstrated upgrade.
- **ASUS Kithara ~481–499k**: real passive planar open headphone and own measured tonal strengths; more expensive and heavier than FT1 PRO, but unique design/pad/mic tradeoffs prevent strict dominance. Require try-on and same-use comparative listening.
- **MDR-MV1 ~498k:** high-frequency fatigue and lighter H6 Air alternative, but different low-end behavior and pro use cases.

### D. >₩500k — future specialists, not automatic exclusions
- **Meze 105 AER (~576k)**: relaxed voicing, long-wear build and replaceable parts.
- **Audeze MM-100 (~639k)**: independent listening verdicts conflict; substantial weight and price. High-value upgrade unproven.
- **Focal Azurys (~664k) / Hadenys (~766.5k)**: premium tonal character and materials; **need actual qualitative benefit beyond less expensive FT1/HD550 before price premium can be justified**.
- **Austrian Audio Hi-X65 (~699k)**, **Sennheiser HD490 PRO (~675k)**: serviceability/studio comfort may add value if these matter; not a price-based rejection.
- **aune SR7000 (~863k)**: highest indexed price among 29, only plausible relative sonic upgrade with lower confidence, so **deep investigation is lower priority** until independent superiority evidence.
- **aune AR5000 MK2 (~538k)**: improved frame, but impedance-spec conflict and scant independent acoustic measurements; expensive to investigate before resolving basic data.
- **FT5 (~568k)**: distinctly warmer/darker planar character might be preferred, though compared with FT1 PRO there is no established proportional improvement.

## Pareto/dominance evaluation

**Formal Pareto-dominated (hard REJECT): 0 of 29.**

It would be wrong to declare formal dominance when (a) actual resale/warranty/stock price is uncertain, (b) resolution, imaging and dynamics remain UNKNOWN relative to H7, (c) open/closed, bass shape, serviceability and wearer comfort are genuinely distinct, or (d) no direct same-condition two-source study establishes inferiority.

This does **not** mean all products have equally good value. It means **research prioritization** and **proof of strict dominance** are different decisions.

## Sensitivity: three scenarios

**Scenario 1 — Oct Sennheiser event still available:** HD560S 189k is unusually compelling due to H7 voice correction potential; HD550 254k adds choice without proven resolution gain; HD600 315k offer is marked sold out, so not an executable deal.

**Scenario 2 — event ended or stock gone:** fallback is historical indexed genuine-category HD560S 216k to regular 229k retail, HD550 indexed ~269k; the entry-price gap shrinks and FT1 216–230k is more attractive for *closed-back* use. New stock evidence required.

**Scenario 3 — premium or card opportunity:** FT1 PRO potentially 285300 won under specific Lotte card terms (Step4 deal record); for eligible member, value improves but still 2026 stock/condition must be confirmed. Focal published long discounts do not become more valuable solely because the list price is crossed out.

## Explicit limitations

- No single percentage performance improvement; no invented ROI per Korean won.
- Step 8 predictions are based mainly on tonal response and subjective listening, not controlled sound-quality ladder or listener measurement.
- 29 PASS do not cover 33 HOLD in same depth. Reassess two HOLD/HIGH HIFIMAN Edition XS and ANANDA NANO only when sealed Korean stock and reliable warranty/price appear.
- Claiming a strict market Pareto frontier is unwarranted on these data. The 'frontier' descriptions are **shortlist hypotheses**, not formally proved physical/acoustic Pareto efficiency.
- The user has not specified a fixed spending ceiling; no such cap is imposed.
- Domestic after-sales costs, pads and serial-specific factory revisions for finalists need confirmation.
- Large open/closed use differences mean a consumer preferring cinema weight in a noisy room may prefer an ostensibly cheaper sealed model.
