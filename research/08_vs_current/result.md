Date: 2026-10-09

## IMPORTANT — This is NOT a measured head-to-head comparison

**The user did not report actually A/B-listening the 29 headphones against the H7, and no independent source provided level-matched all-model comparisons using the actual AE-5 analog headphone output.**

Therefore the change vectors are **predicted directional changes from a cross-source synthesis**, not claims of tested hearing improvements. Even a coded `UP2` means "relatively strong expectation of improved feature compared with the reference," **not** a measured two-step improvement, percentage gain, or guaranteed audible outcome. If references were incompatible or unknown, use `UNKNOWN` rather than a fabricated number.

Three source categories:
1. **Direct passive H7 tonal measurement**: E0006, refreshed by E0353 (DIY-Audio-Heaven). The reviewer reports ~5 dB warm-bass emphasis near 80–300Hz and steep 200 Hz–3 kHz descent. This specific frequency response is *not* the same as H7's separate USB DAC/DSP mode, and the H7 uses an in-line analog volume control.
2. **Individual candidate sound measurements and expert listening**: Step 6 E0227–E0288, cataloged in [acoustic_matrix.csv](../06_audio_analysis/acoustic_matrix.csv).
3. **Long-term comfort/fit, QC, official parts**: Step 7 E0289–E0352, cataloged in [comfort_qc_matrix.csv](../07_comfort_quality/comfort_qc_matrix.csv).

Additional refreshed H7 support/subjective sources E0354 and E0355. Audio sources are not guaranteed to use the same ear simulator, coupler, compensation target, wearing pressure, ear pads, level, room or source chain. Never plot them on one fake exact dB scale.

## Interpretive codebook

- `↑↑` / `UP2`: **stronger predicted positive difference**, especially tonal midrange difference because H7 passive response is clearly recessed. Must be independently validated by matched audition.
- `↑` / `UP1`: modest plausible positive difference or presentation change, not a promise of sufficient upgrade.
- `→` / `SAME`: expected little change only with appropriate evidence; **none forced merely because unknown**.
- `↓` / `DOWN1`: plausible negative difference; if wear-dependent, leave comfort UNKNOWN and write a risk note instead.
- `?` / `UNKNOWN`: no sufficiently transferable proof; not evidence of equality.
- Bass quantity `MORE/LESS/SAME/UNKNOWN`: **amount**, not a quality score.
- Confidence MEDIUM: relatively robust source cross-check of *the direction*, but still **not** direct A/B; LOW: source conflicts, missing independent lab or high wearer variability; UNKNOWN: model version is not fixed.
- Price deliberately excluded.

**Deliberately UNKNOWN for all 29:** H7 relative resolution, separation, imaging, dynamics, and wearer-specific long-term comfort. Cross-source FR or a phrase such as "detailed" is insufficient to quantify these phenomena. Directional stage/tonal balance only where independently grounded.

## Key case analysis

### A. Sennheiser HD 560S / HD 550 / HD 505 / HD 600

All four are likely to present vocals/acoustic material more naturally than the measured H7 passive lower-mid-to-presence profile. The biggest apparent gain could be the simple *tonal correction* of recessed H7 mids; it **must not be sold as inherently more microscopic resolution**. HD600 favors vocal timbre but not deep movie-bass or very wide stage; HD550 and HD505 are modern alternate tunings, not proven uniformly above HD560S. A user's existing H7 bass-heavy movie enjoyment and closed-room isolation are advantages the open Sennheisers cannot preserve.

**Decision:** all remain meaningful H7 sound-change research options, with individual listening/tone preference and comfort yet to establish actual replacement merit.

### B. Audio-Technica ATH-R50x / ATH-R70xa

R50x's fairly neutral measured mids versus H7's recessed mids support predicted clearer voices, while the ~6–10kHz emphasis can masquerade as "resolution" and may cause fatigue. R70xa may offer excellent balanced open sound but 470Ω electrical level/headroom and unusual cup articulation must be verified. **No proven technical resolution upgrade number**.

**Decision:** vocal potential positive, all the following UNKNOWN: controlled imaging, actual ear comfort, guaranteed treble-quality improvement.

### C. Sony MDR-M1 / MDR-MV1 / INZONE H6 Air

- **MDR-M1:** remains a sealed headphone with possible improved bass texture and cinematic bass relative to H7. Yet its depressed ~4kHz presence and ear contact for some heads mean it is **not** treated as a clear across-the-board vocal + comfort upgrade.
- **MDR-MV1:** spatially open with unusually extended bass, but prominent upper treble/sibilance creates fatigue risk on voices and animation, and open design reduces shared-room isolation.
- **INZONE H6 Air:** light, breathable, passive analog, plausible voice/open-stage improvement for ordinary games. Shared Sony driver genealogy does **not** mean it exactly equals more expensive MDR-MV1 in acoustic performance.

### D. FiiO FT1 / FT1 PRO — genuine sound upgrade versus QC

- **FT1** can preserve closed isolation and fuller cinema bass while improving vocal balance and bass quality relative to the old H7 passive presentation. Its strong own bass means a genuinely *dramatic* vocal contrast is not yet certain.
- **FT1 PRO** is a distinct open planar variant with leaner bass quantity, broader stage and likely stronger vocals compared with H7 passive. It does not preserve bass slam/isolation. Both models have **manufacturer-confirmed early-batch structural QC issues**: see Step 7. Possible upgrade does not mean unconditionally safe purchase; actual Korean serial/repair channels should be checked.

### E. Premium audio candidates: HD490 PRO, Meze, Focal, Audeze, ASUS

The H7 passive upper-mid recession creates tonal upgrade headroom for many products regardless of their sticker price, but price is reserved for Step 9. In particular:
- **Focal Hadenys** may sound substantially more natural on dialogue/music; specific narrow-cup/clamp complaints limit replacement appeal for some users.
- **ASUS ROG Kithara** is a passive planar open headset and can make a meaningful tuning/stage change; 420g mass and fit tradeoff could make it worse for very long sessions.
- **Audeze MM-100** earns a very cautious `UNCERTAIN` rather than forced acoustic rank because RTINGS and SoundGuys listening conclusions differ significantly. Music/film gains are not established by one lab's high aggregate score.
- **FiiO FT3** is marked `INSUFFICIENT_VARIANT_DATA`: the 32Ω and 350Ω versions use different drivers and tuning; one C017 record cannot claim a specific measured change without choosing an exact SKU.
- **Shure SRH440A/SRH840A** are `SMALL_OR_UNCERTAIN` for *upgrade confidence*, not proven lower sound quality, because measurements actually pertaining to the new A versions are too sparse.

## Physical use versus sonic change

- H7's original long-session comfort and closed-back isolation are **positive** baselines, not automatic negatives.
- No matched-user wearing test was performed; therefore every `H7_Comfort_Delta=UNKNOWN`.
- Extended-wear discomfort risks by model (MDR-M1 shallow pads, ATH-R70xa cup-angle restriction, Hadenys clamp/ear clearance, FT1 mechanical batch defects, Kithara heavy wear) are displayed in risk text and previous matrix instead of fictitious DOWN scores.
- Open versus closed isolation is treated as a discrete **capability trade-off**, not a "soundstage improved, therefore all games and films improve" rule.
- Future side-by-side auditions should set the H7's analog in-line volume control to a known stable position, avoid excessive levels, match perceived SPL and use **the same test recordings / AE-5 Direct Mode**.

## Evidence limitations: why some vectors are UNKNOWN

1. Cross-device frequency response measurement cannot demonstrate separate resolution/dynamics numbers.
2. "More open stage" can be a presentation change, but stable imaging accuracy requires better method.
3. Film benefit varies with soundtrack low-frequency content, room isolation and preferences.
4. H7 USB-mode reviews cannot be used as the actual AE-5 passive mode.
5. Current H7 user's own subjective A/B report is not supplied, so directional grades are **probabilistic hypotheses**, not what this user has actually heard.
6. Some newer or A-suffix products lack two independent same-version sources.
7. H7 passive baseline itself comes from a limited single model measurement setup; product aging/inline volume setting may differ.
